Mission Control — Growth Command Center

Goal of this module: find target accounts in a given topic/country/region, screen them, reach out on the same platform, convert to phone/email, make direct contact, invite to an online meeting and, once approved, continue the journey — or, on parallel tracks: create a job posting + landing page, or receive and screen resumes from LinkedIn/Indeed. Every target group (freelance beauty service providers, beauty/investment/business buy-sell influencers, bank network, contacts close to the plan) has its own policy and route. Work through the five steps below in order; each step operates directly on the system's real data (Presentation Campaigns / Prospect Outreach).

Step 1 — Define target audience groups (goal, commission, route, regions, policy)

Step 2 — What each route does

1. Direct network search & outreach

Search a platform by topic/country/region → screen matches (score ≥80%) → contact inside the platform → convert to email/phone → contact directly → invite to an online meeting → if approved, move forward to contract.

2. Job posting + landing page

Publish a job ad + a single-page site (your text or ours, hosted wherever you choose) → screen applicants → contact → invite (address/name shown on the page) → same approval flow as above.

3. Resume intake (LinkedIn/Indeed)

Pull submitted resumes from LinkedIn/Indeed/manual links → download & read → screen → continue exactly like route 2 from there.

Step 3 — Steps for this group's route (add/remove message-script segments)

Step 4 — Regions & policy

Set per-group in Step 1's form above: target regions, daily contact cap, and allowed working hours.

Step 5 — Run & see results

Activating a group marks it live; open Pipeline Overview to watch prospects move through every step in real time.
